FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about CampusXchange.

What is CampusXchange?

CampusXchange is a student-focused marketplace where students can buy, sell and exchange useful items within their campus community.

What can I sell on CampusXchange?

Students can list books, calculators, electronics, cycles, accessories and other useful college-related items.

Is CampusXchange only for students?

Yes. The platform is designed primarily for students and campus communities.

How do I contact a seller?

Open the product details and use the Contact Seller or Message Seller option.

Is online payment available?

Currently, CampusXchange focuses on connecting students. Online payment can be integrated in a future version.
